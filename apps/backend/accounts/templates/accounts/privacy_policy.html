Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026

Symbio is a nutrition-tracking app operated by Illia Dovhoshliubyi (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the Symbio app and the symbio.drakon4ik.uk service collect, why, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. It applies to the mobile app and to this website.

Data we collect

Account data

When you create an account we store your email address (your login identity), an optional display name and username, and your password — stored only as a salted cryptographic hash, never in plain text. We use your email address solely to operate your account: verification, password reset, and account-deletion confirmation emails. We do not send marketing email.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and name with us to create or match your account. We never see your Google password, and we do not request access to anything else in your Google account. Google's own handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Health and nutrition data

The core of Symbio is the food diary you keep: the foods you log, their amounts and nutritional values, meal types, the times you log them, your daily calorie and nutrient goals, and any custom foods you create (including photos you choose to upload for them). This is health-related data and we treat it accordingly: it is used only to show you your own history and statistics, it is never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never sold.

Device timezone

The app reports your device's timezone (for example “Europe/Kyiv”) so that meals you log group into the correct calendar days on your wall clock. We store only the timezone name, not your location — we collect no GPS or location data.

Camera and barcode scanning

The app uses your camera only to scan food barcodes. Scanning happens entirely on your device: camera images are processed locally and never leave your phone, are never uploaded, and are never stored. Only the decoded barcode number is sent to our server and to Open Food Facts to look up the product.

Crash reports and diagnostics

To find and fix bugs, the app reports crashes and errors to Sentry. A crash report contains technical details such as your device model, operating system version, app version, and the technical trace of the error. Crash reports do not include the contents of your food diary or your goals.

Third-party services

Symbio uses three third-party services, listed below. Beyond these, your data is not shared with anyone. We show no ads and we do not sell or rent data to anyone.

  • Open Food Facts — food and nutrition data comes from the Open Food Facts database. Product lookups send the barcode number or your search text to Open Food Facts; they include nothing that identifies you or your account.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose it as your login method, as described above.
  • Sentry — crash reporting, as described above.

Storage and security

Your data is stored on our servers and is encrypted in transit: the app talks to the server exclusively over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Access to production data is limited to the operator.

Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When your account is deleted, all data associated with it — your profile, food diary, goals, preferences, and custom foods — is permanently deleted from our systems at the same time. Crash reports in Sentry expire automatically per Sentry's standard retention period (90 days).

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Profile → Delete account. You will be asked to re-enter your password (or re-confirm with Google) before deletion.
  • On the web, without the app: symbio.drakon4ik.uk/delete-account — enter your account email and we send a single-use confirmation link.

Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile and preferences in the app, and delete everything as described above. For access or data-export requests, or any question about this policy, contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Symbio is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 13 and over.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app.
